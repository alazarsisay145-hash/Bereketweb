Our place

A fresh corner of Hawassa

Bereket Juice & Salad is a family-run juice house near the shores of Lake Hawassa. Fruit arrives from local farms every morning — mangoes, avocados, papayas and bananas — and leaves the counter as layered spris, cold smoothies and generous fruit salads.

Come for a quick fresh-pressed glass, stay for a burger and the lake breeze.

  • Open every day7:00 AM – 9:00 PM
